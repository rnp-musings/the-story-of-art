# Story of Art: proposed coverage of all states and union territories

**Status:** proposal for review. Nothing here has been researched or built yet.
**Marks:** ✓ = already on the site (64 entries). **Bold** = Tier 1 (build first). Plain = Tier 2. *Italic* = Tier 3 (build last, or leave for community contribution).

## How "all" is defined
"All art forms of India" has no fixed edge, so this list aims for **at least 3 to 6 entries per state and union territory**, covering every type (painting, textile, craft, sculpture, dance, music, theatre and puppetry, martial art, ritual art). Where a form is shared by several states, it is listed once, under the place most tied to it, and its map shows every region.

## Size
Roughly **200 new entries** on top of the 64 already built, so a site of about **265**. Tier 1 (bold) is about **100 entries**; this alone gives every state and union territory at least 2 entries, and no region left empty.

## How I would build it
1. **Tier 1 first**, state by state, in batches of about 6 entries per research agent, with sources shown on each page, as for Kerala.
2. **A review pass for every batch.** The earlier music and dance batches show that automated research leaves gaps. Each batch should go to someone who knows the region before the site is promoted.
3. **Photos.** Wikimedia has been slow and patchy. For the north-east, Goa, the islands and tribal arts it will be thin. Photos from you, from artists' groups or from museums are better and avoid licensing risk.
4. **Tier 3 as a community-contributions layer** rather than something we write, so that artists and scholars can correct and add entries.
5. **Quality bar.** Each entry gets a source list; claims we could not verify are left out; honours and dates are only stated if a source supports them.
